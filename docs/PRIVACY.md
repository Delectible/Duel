# Duel privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Duel is an app for playing card games against other people online or
against a bot. To run online games it keeps a small amount of data about
you. It does not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics
trackers.

**Games against the bot** run entirely on your phone. Nothing about them
is sent anywhere.

**Your account.** To play online you create an account. We keep:

- your **username** and an **account ID**, so other players can find and
  play you;
- your **email address**, only if you sign in with email or with a Google
  or Apple account, to sign you in and recover your account. It is never
  shown to other players.

**Your games.** For online games we keep the matches you play: who played,
the moves, the result, and when. This is needed to run the game between
two phones, settle results and show your match history and stats. Your
opponent sees your username and your moves in games you play together.

**Notifications.** If you turn on notifications (for example "it's your
turn"), your phone gives us a notification token so we can send them. You
can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

**Where it's kept.** Account and game data is stored on the servers of the
cloud provider that runs Duel's online games, which processes it only on
our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS). We
don't share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

**Crash reports.** If the app crashes, details may be kept on your phone
to help fix the problem. They are not sent anywhere unless you choose to
send one to us.

**How long we keep it.** Your account and match history are kept while
your account exists. When you delete your account they are deleted within
30 days. Games you played may still show your opponent that they played
"a deleted player".

<a id="deleting-your-account"></a>
**Deleting your account.** Delete it in the app (Settings > Delete
account, once accounts arrive), or ask us to delete it by opening an
issue at https://github.com/Delectible/Duel/issues with your username
(don't post your email address there). Uninstalling the app removes
everything it stored on your phone; it does not delete your online
account.

**Children.** Duel is not directed at children under 13 and we don't
knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made an
account, tell us and we'll delete it.

**Changes.** If what we collect changes, we'll update this page and the
date at the top.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/Delectible/Duel/issues.
